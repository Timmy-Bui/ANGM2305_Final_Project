Asteroid Destroyer in simple terms is just a sci fi game where you can choose what ship to pick and what weapons as well and just destory the asteroid for scores and just stay alive.
    There are four ships and four weapons types with their own stats right now but more could be added if needed.

There is a active hp that is linked to the ship stats that show at the top left corner as well the score, what ship type and weapon type you have shown. There is also a pause button to just pause the game.

There are main menu, ship selection screen, weapon selection screen, and the game screen which the most important. There are also back button to click on for some of the screens
